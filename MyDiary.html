🌸 My First Blog

Date: 18 July 2025

Hello friends,
I’m not really sure how to start this, but I’m truly excited to begin this journey.

I have always loved nature, and I have a deep passion for gardening. Planting, taking care of plants, and simply admiring their beauty brings me so much happiness.

Every time I look at my plants, I feel like capturing their photos, sharing them with others, and talking about them.

That’s why, two years ago, I created an Instagram account and a YouTube channel dedicated to my plants.
I worked really hard on them, but I couldn’t stay consistent because I was pursuing myB.Sc. in Botany at M.J.P. Rohilkhand University, Bareilly.

I love gardening, but my passion was something else...
I always dreamed of entering the IT field.
However, due to some reasons, I couldn’t take admission at that time.

But now, I have no regrets.
Everything is falling into place beautifully.

Now I’m following my passion for gardening too — through my small terrace garden and these lovely social media platforms. 🌿📸
And yes...
I’ve also taken admission in MCA at Teerthanker Mahaveer University, Moradabad!

I’m really happy with my decision and satisfied with where I am today. My MCA classes haven’t started yet, and I don’t know coding either...

But I really wanted to build a website on my own.apne liye.
I didn’t even have a laptop...
Still, with just my mobile and the help of ChatGPT, I created my website:

🎉 Arshmi Garden Diaries 🎉

I’m super excited about this new journey and eagerly waiting for my MCA classes to begin.
