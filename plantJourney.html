🌿 A Plant I Couldn’t Let Go

Date: 20 July 2025

I had a beautiful Tecoma plant with bright orange flowers. It was quite healthy and blooming really well.

But since I have many plants in my garden and not much space, I decided to give some of them to my best friend.
I even told my mom that I would give this Tecoma to her, and she agreed.

It was the rainy season… it had been raining heavily for several days. One day, I suddenly noticed that my Tecoma was starting to wilt. But I didn’t pay much attention to it at first, and I let 2–3 days pass like that.

When I saw that its condition was getting worse, I realized that I needed to do something.

So, I immediately took it out of the pot… and I saw that it had root rot 😞 I felt really sad and even thought - maybe it got upset with me, because I kept talking about giving it away…

When I checked, almost 90% of its roots were damaged. Looking at its condition, there was hardly any hope that it would survive. But still, I treated it with full belief that it would recover.

It took me 2 days… but I did everything I could.

Now it’s been around 15–20 days since then… and today I saw that My Tecoma is alive! 💚 It has even started showing new growth 🌱

I’m so happy. And now I’ve decided: I will never talk about giving it away again. Instead, I’ll gift my bestie a new plant. 🌸

Date: 20 July 2025

🛠️ Tecoma Treatment Steps

• I removed the plant from the pot and gently cut away all the damaged roots. ✅

• I placed the roots in water for a few minutes to clean them, then took them out and let them dry. ✅

• I spread the soil from the pot on the surface and allowed it to dry as well. ✅

• Then, I applied turmeric powder to the roots and kept the plant in a shaded area for a day. I also sprinkled turmeric on the soil and placed it in sunlight. ✅

• The next day, I repotted the plant in the same pot with the same soil and kept it in shade for 3–4 days, protecting it from rain. ✅

• Once the weather improved, I moved it back to the terrace. Now it is completely fine and recovering well. 🌼

🌱 Common Plant Queries

1. How often should I water my plants?
💧 Most plants need watering once every 1–3 days. Check the topsoil – if it's dry 1 inch deep, it's time to water.
💧 ज़्यादातर पौधों को हर 1–3 दिन में पानी देना चाहिए। अगर मिट्टी ऊपर से 1 इंच तक सूखी हो, तो पानी देना ज़रूरी है।

2. Why are my plant leaves turning yellow?
🍂 Overwatering, poor drainage, or lack of sunlight. Check pot drainage and move plant to sunlight.

🪴 Use well-draining mix: garden soil + compost + sand/cocopeat for healthy growth.

5. How can I protect my plants from pests naturally?
